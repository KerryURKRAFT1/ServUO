//================STATIC HOUSE SYSTEM================//
		HOW IT WORKS (FOR GMs)

- Add the sign with [add Statichousesign (target the ground near the wall) – the sign is flippable.

- House Name: The name you want to assign to the house – the player will be able to change it.

- Sale Price: Sale price in gold (e.g., 50k → 50000).

- APPLY button – Applies the changes made on this specific page of the gump.

//================MANAGE AREA================//


Here you set the area of the house where players can lock down objects on the ground.

IMPORTANT:

- Go to the top-left (North-West) corner of the house. Use [Where to get coordinates.

- Note the X and Y of that tile.

-  W is the number of tiles from the top-left going right. (Est)

-  H is the number of tiles from the top-left going down. (South

IMPORTANT :
If you’re standing on tile (X, Y), The tile you're standing on is ALWAYS included both when extending eastward (W) and southward (H).
Setting W = 1 and H = 1 → will cover only that tile

- Pressing Add will add the designated area to the house.
- You can add multiple areas for houses with irregular shapes.

Example

LLLLL
LL        <---- EXAMPLE HOUSE SHAPE
LL

In this case, two areas will be defined:

- One for the 5 Ls on top

- One for the 4 Ls at the bottom
